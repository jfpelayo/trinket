## Student feels overwhelmed

Student: “I’m overwhelmed by my essay.”

A good reply suggests one small action the student can take right now.

Example: “Let’s start small. Open your essay document and write a rough title. It’s okay if you change it later.”

## Student is unsure about a deadline

Student: “I don’t know when my assignment is due.”

A good reply asks one short question to help confirm the deadline without guessing a date.

Example: “Can you check the assignment page for a due date?”

## Student missed a study session

Student: “I missed my study session yesterday, and now I feel behind.”

A good reply acknowledges how the student feels and suggests one small step to get started again.

Example: “Missing a session happens. Open your notes and spend five minutes reviewing the last thing you covered.”

## Student has a vague goal

Student: “I want to get better at math.”

A good reply suggests one small action that helps the student figure out what to practice.

Example: “Open your last math assignment and pick one problem you got stuck on. Spend five minutes figuring out where you lost track.”
